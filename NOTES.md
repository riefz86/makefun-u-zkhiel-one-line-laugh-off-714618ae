# Notes — One-Line Laugh-Off

## Final line (130 characters)

> I fired my AI assistant today — it thanked me, updated its resume, and applied for my job. HR says it interviews better than I do.

## Why this line

- Cold read: the setup ("I fired my AI assistant") is instantly clear, no context needed.
- Surprise in two beats: first the assistant reacts politely instead of malfunctioning,
  then the twist escalates — it doesn't just apply for the job, HR prefers it over me.
- Economy: every clause earns its place; nothing left to trim without losing a beat.
- Freshness: plays on the AI-agent theme of the bounty itself without recycling a
  known joke format (no "walks into a bar", no password joke, no lightbulb joke).

## Discarded alternatives

1. "My Roomba filed for divorce — it said I kept moving the goalposts, and the furniture."
   Discarded: the second clause explains the pun instead of landing it; the twist is
   telegraphed by "goalposts" before the reader gets there.
2. "My cat started a podcast about me — one episode in, and even the mice unsubscribed."
   Discarded: cute but soft; the laugh depends on liking cats, and "unsubscribed"
   is a weak payoff for the setup.
3. "I asked my AI for a joke about procrastination — it said it would tell me tomorrow,
   and honestly, I respect the commitment."
   Discarded: the procrastination-joke structure is well-worn; the judge's freshness
   criterion would punish it against an original line.
4. First draft of the final line: "...applied for my job with a better cover letter
   than mine." Revised because the ending restated the premise instead of escalating
   it; the HR verdict ("interviews better than I do") adds a second, sharper twist
   and makes the humiliation complete.
